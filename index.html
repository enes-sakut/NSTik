Your privacy at a glance

NSTik is provided by Enes Sakut. Your downloaded media and library are stored on your device. We use service providers to resolve media links, measure app usage and verify purchases. This policy explains those different data flows.

02

Media, clipboard and local storage

When you paste or import a TT link, NSTik uses that link to retrieve the content you request. Clipboard access is subject to iOS permissions; the app does not send your entire clipboard to analytics. The requested source link is sent to the TikWM resolution service at tikwm.com. That service and the media hosts/CDNs receive network information, including your IP address, and the requests needed to deliver thumbnails, video, audio or slides. Their processing is governed by their own policies. Do not submit private or sensitive links.

Downloaded files, thumbnails, source URLs, descriptions, creator metadata and playlists are stored locally to display your library without repeated downloads. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Sharing or exporting a file sends it to the destination you select.

03

App usage analytics

NSTik uses Google Analytics for Firebase. Analytics is enabled in the current app and records screen names and bounded events such as imports, download results, playback actions, playlist actions, plan selection and purchase/restore outcomes. Technical information can include app version, operating system, device model, app-instance identifiers, language, sessions and approximate country/region derived from network information. We use this to understand usage and improve the app.

Our analytics events do not include pasted links, media titles, descriptions, creator names, playlist names, local file paths or media files. IDFA and IDFV collection are disabled in our integration; precise GPS location is not requested. There is currently no in-app analytics opt-out control. You can contact us about your data rights. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

04

Purchases and persistent device records

Apple processes App Store payments. NSTik does not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat helps us verify purchases, restore access and determine Pro entitlement. It processes an app-user identifier, transaction/product and subscription information, entitlement status and technical information needed for this service. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

We store the RevenueCat customer identifier and a verified entitlement snapshot in the iOS Keychain. These records can remain after uninstalling the app, helping reconnect purchase access after reinstalling on the same device. The cached record alone does not permanently grant Pro: entitlement is verified with RevenueCat. Uninstalling does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete provider transaction records.

05

Purposes, sharing and retention

We process requested links and purchase information to provide the service and fulfil purchases; usage information helps us maintain and improve it. Where applicable, processing relies on contractual necessity, legitimate interests in operating and improving the service, legal obligations for transaction records, or consent where required by law. This policy does not replace consent where consent is legally required.

Data is shared with the providers described above for those purposes, or where legally required or necessary to protect rights and security. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Providers may process data outside your country under their applicable transfer arrangements.

Local files remain until deleted from the app or its storage, subject to backups and separate Keychain records. Provider data is retained under the applicable service configuration, operational needs and legal requirements; we do not promise that uninstalling removes remote analytics or transaction records. Contact us for retention or deletion information relating to your data.

06

Your choices and rights

You can delete downloaded files and playlists in NSTik, control clipboard and export permissions through iOS, and manage subscriptions in Apple settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where applicable, and complain to your data-protection authority. Email us to make a request. We may need proportionate information to verify and locate relevant records. Do not send payment-card details or sensitive media. Some records must be retained for legal obligations, and data kept only on your device may not be accessible to us.

07

Children, security and this website

NSTik is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age required in your country. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate. We use platform protections and encrypted network connections, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

These legal pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or optional cookies. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which can process network/access information to serve the site; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
