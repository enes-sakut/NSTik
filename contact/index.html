Help us understand the issue

When contacting us, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The NSTik version you are using.
  • What happened and the steps that led to it.
  • A screenshot of any error, if helpful.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or private media.

Purchases and subscriptions

If Pro access is missing, try Restore in the NSTik paywall using the Apple Account that made the purchase. You can manage subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

For help with purchase access, email us at the address above.
